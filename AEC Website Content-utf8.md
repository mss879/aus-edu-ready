## <a id="_Toc220502411"></a>Your Step\-by\-Step Journey to PR

__Step 1: Study in Australia__  
Complete your degree at an Australian university or college \(2\-4 years\)

__Step 2: 485 Graduate Visa__  
Get 2\-4 years work rights after graduation depending on your qualification

__Step 3: Gain Experience__  
Professional Year program \+ Australian work experience \+ build PR points

__Step 4: Apply for PR__  
You may apply for the __Skilled Work Regional \(Subclass 491\)__ visa, which offers a pathway to permanent residency\. Alternatively, you can pursue __direct PR options__, including the __Skilled Independent \(Subclass 189\)__, __State Nominated \(Subclass 190\)__, or __Employer Nomination Scheme \(Subclass 186\)__ visas\.

__Timeline:__ Total journey typically 4\-6 years from student visa to PR

## <a id="_Toc220502412"></a>Permanent Residency Categories

__Category 1: Skilled Work Regional \(Subclass 491\) __  
ÔÇó State or family sponsorship required  
ÔÇó Points\-based system \(65\+ points including nomination\)  
ÔÇó Live, work, and study in designated regional areas  
ÔÇó Pathway to PR after 3 years  
ÔÇó Includes partner and children

__Category 2: Skilled Independent \(Subclass 189\)__  
ÔÇó Points\-based system \(65\+ points required\)  
ÔÇó No sponsor required  
ÔÇó Live and work anywhere in Australia  
ÔÇó Includes partner and children

__Category 3: Skilled Nominated \(Subclass 190\)__  
ÔÇó State/territory government nomination  
ÔÇó 65\+ points including nomination  
ÔÇó Commitment to live in the nominating state for 2 years  
ÔÇó Includes partner and children

__Category 4: Employer Nomination Scheme \(Subclass 186\)__  
ÔÇó Sponsored by an Australian employer  
ÔÇó Pathway after working on a temporary visa \(e\.g\. 482\)  
ÔÇó Permanent residency from day one  
ÔÇó Includes partner and children

## <a id="_Toc220502413"></a>Frequently Asked Questions \- PR Pathways

__Q: Can I bring my spouse while I study in Australia?__  
Yes\! Your spouse can accompany you on a dependent visa\. They get work rights \(48 hours per fortnight during semester, unlimited during breaks\)\. We handle all dependent visa applications\.

__Q: How long does it take from student visa to PR?__  
Typically 4\-6 years: 2 years study \+ 1\-2 years on 485 visa gaining experience \+ 1\-2 years PR processing\. We guide you through the entire journey\.

__Q: Do I need work experience before getting PR?__  
Australian work experience significantly increases your PR points\. Your 485 visa gives you time to gain this experience\. Our job placement services help you find relevant work\.

__Q: What is a Professional Year Program?__  
A 12\-month program combining formal learning and internship for Accounting, IT, or Engineering graduates\. Adds 5 PR points \+ Australian work experience\. We arrange placements\.

__Q: Can my family get PR if I get it?__  
Yes\! Your spouse and dependent children under 23 are included in your PR application\. Once you have PR, they also become permanent residents with full rights\.

__Q: Which courses give the best PR chances?__  
Occupations on the skilled occupation list have better PR prospects: Nursing, Engineering, IT, Accounting, Teaching, Trades \(electrician, plumber, carpenter\)\. We guide you to PR\-friendly courses\.

<a id="_Toc220502420"></a>__Frequently Asked Questions \- Partner Visas__

__Q: My partner doesn't speak English\. Can they still come?__  
Yes\! There's no English requirement for dependent partners on student visas\. They can learn English after arriving in Australia through free or low\-cost community classes\.

__Q: We're not married but in a relationship\. Can my partner come?__
